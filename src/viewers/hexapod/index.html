One finger rotates. Pinch to zoom; move two fingers to pan. Tap a part to inspect it.

Hexapod MKII by Cornell Physical Intelligence and GeoData.

This model is intended for testing balance and finding effective walking policies in simulators such as NVIDIA Isaac Sim. Simulating foot contact, friction, and ground forces helps evaluate how the robot walks and stays stable.
